You're locked inside a MASSIVE abandoned supermarket. By day, scavenge the aisles for food, batteries and building materials. By night, the lights go out... and THE LOCUST starts hunting.

It hears your footsteps. It sees your flashlight. And every night it gets faster, smarter and stronger.

LOOT - BUILD - SURVIVE
- A huge store that is different on every server: food court, electronics, freezers, a dark basement and more
- Build a base: walls, doors, beds, generators, traps and turrets
- Push shopping carts, hide in lockers, revive your friends
- Play SOLO or bring a PARTY of up to 4 into your own private store
- Modes: Survival, Infection, Hardcore
- Daily missions, levels, outfits and titles

Tips:
- Running is loud. Crouch to sneak.
- Turn your flashlight off when it's close.
- Never go out alone at night.

How many nights can you survive?
